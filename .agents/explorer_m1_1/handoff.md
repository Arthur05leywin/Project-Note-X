## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Premise:** The project's goal is to ensure comprehensive coverage of all regional and general anatomical questions from the `2010-25 1ST PROFF PYQS` PDF (distilled into `anatomy_pyq_text.txt`).
2. **Analysis Method:** The four target HTML files were parsed and matched against the specific regional segments of the PYQ text (Upper Limb, Lower Limb, Thorax, General Anatomy/Embryology/Genetics/Histology).
3. **Identification of Gaps:**
   - Gaps were identified where key questions (especially multi-part essay questions or common "Explain Why" questions) had no corresponding section, card, or mention in the respective HTML files.
   - Topics like "Arches of the Foot", "Structures under Gluteus Maximus", "Embryological Development of Cardiac Septa", and "General Embryology/Genetics/Histology" represent major, high-weight areas (frequently carrying 7 to 15 marks) that are completely absent from the current module files.
4. **Actionable Recommendations:** By defining precise insertion points (e.g. inserting Embryology/Genetics as new sections in Module 1, or inserting Arches of the Foot as a new section after Leg Muscles in Module 3), the team can systematically implement these missing topics to achieve 100% PYQ coverage alignment.

## 3. Caveats

- **Scope Limits:** This audit is restricted to Modules 1, 2, 3, and 4. Subsequent modules (Module 5: Abdomen, Module 6: Pelvis, Module 7: Head & Neck, Module 8: Neuroanatomy) were not audited but likely contain similar gaps based on the presence of these topics in the latter sections of the PYQ text.
- **Histology Placement:** Some systemic histology topics (like classical hepatic lobule, spleen/lymph node, thyroid) are listed in the General Anatomy PYQ section. We have recommended placing them in Module 1 or their respective regional modules (e.g. liver in Abdomen, thyroid in Head/Neck).
