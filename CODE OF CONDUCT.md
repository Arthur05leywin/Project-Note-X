# Project X Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
[cite_start]We are dedicated to providing a high-utility, harassment-free study environment for medical students prepping under severe exam panic[cite: 209].

## Our Standards
* [cite_start]**Academic Honesty:** Explicitly preserve product boundaries (e.g., maintaining that this system optimizes revision and does not replace primary textbooks)[cite: 395].
* [cite_start]**Collaboration:** Foster helpful peer interactions across auxiliary distribution pathways (e.g., connected Telegram support groups)[cite: 427].
* **Zero Tolerances:** Toxic behavior, spamming external product checkouts, or degrading user focus during high-stress exam timelines is subject to immediate channel blocking.
