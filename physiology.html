Examiners give marks for structured flow. Every major pathway gets a mobile-safe, properly labelled HTML flowchart — no SVG hacks, no rotated text.

Collapsible Q&A at the end of every section — framed exactly like a professor's follow-up question, with the answer structured for a 2-minute viva response.

Group-D EQ templates for every major topic. Definition → Mechanism → Clinical significance → Examiner tip. Write the template once; score every time.
