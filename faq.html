Common questions

Before you start.

Is this enough for WBUHS exams?

It's a high-yield revision layer built from repeated PYQ patterns. Use alongside your notes and core concepts — it's designed to compress what you've already learned.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Built mobile-first for hostel rooms and last-minute review sessions. Dark mode, collapsible sections, one-handed scrolling.

Is it based on actual PYQs?

Yes. Every topic is tagged with its WBUHS appearance history from 2010-S through 2025 — frequency, year patterns, and trap patterns.

Is this a replacement for textbooks?

No. It's a recall and prioritization system for revision, not a primary learning resource.

Are viva questions included?

Yes. Version X modules include spoken-answer prompts designed for viva and practical prep.
